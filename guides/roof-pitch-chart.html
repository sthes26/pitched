Roof pitch chart

Short answer: roof pitch is inches of rise per 12 inches of run. A 6/12 roof rises 6 inches every foot, which is 26.6° or a 50% grade. Multiply the run by the rafter factor to get the sloped length.

Example: a 6/12 roof with a 14 ft run has a rafter about 14 × 1.118 = 15.65 ft long, before overhang. Roof area works the same way: plan area × rafter factor = sloped area. Divide by 100 to get squares.

Metal roofs vary by panel and seam type, and every manufacturer lists its own minimum. Follow the one in the installation instructions.

These are the model code numbers. Your state or city may have adopted a different edition or made amendments. Always confirm requirements with your building official.

How to measure roof pitch

  1. With a level and tape: hold a level flat against a rafter or the roof deck, mark 12 inches along it, and measure straight down to the roof. That number is the rise.
  2. From the attic: measure from the top of a rafter at a 12 inch level line, same as above. Safer than the roof.
  3. With your phone: in Pitched, tap Measure and lay the phone on the rafter or a straightedge on the roof. It reads the pitch, degrees and percent. Calibrate once first so a case or camera bump doesn't skew it.
