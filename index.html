Getting accurate readings

  • Calibrate first. Pitched asks the first time you measure. Lay the phone on any flat, steady spot (it doesn't need to be level), then turn it end for end as prompted. It takes about 20 seconds and cancels out a case or camera bump. Calibrate again if you change cases.
  • Lay the phone on the surface, not on shingle tabs or a lumpy spot. On a rafter or a straightedge works best.
  • Edge mode: stand the phone on its long edge to read a rafter or stringer.
  • Check critical slopes with a level. Pitched is a measuring aid.

About the code checks

Stair, ramp and roof checks use the model codes (2018, 2021 or 2024 IRC and IBC, and the 2010 ADA Standards). Your state or city may have adopted a different edition or made amendments. Always confirm requirements with your building official.
