Privacy Policy

Pitched: Roof, Stair & Ramp, by STA Design LLC. Effective November 9, 2026.

Short version: Pitched does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app does not send anything to our servers. We don't have any.

What stays on your phone

Pitched uses your iPhone's motion sensors to read slope. Readings are worked out on the phone and are not saved or sent anywhere. The app saves a few settings on your phone so it remembers them: your calibration, roof type, slope units, code edition, and stair and ramp type. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you tap Share, iOS's share sheet sends the text or picture only to the app or person you choose. Pitched doesn't see or keep a copy.

Purchases

Pitched Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether this Apple ID owns Pro.

Children

Pitched does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example a feature that saves job addresses, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
