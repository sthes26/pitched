Privacy Policy

Pitched: Roof, Stair & Ramp, by STA Design LLC. Effective November 22, 2026.

Short version: Pitched does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app does not send anything to our servers. We don't have any. Jobs, photos and your company profile are saved only on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Pitched uses your iPhone's motion sensors to read slope. Readings are worked out on the phone and are not saved or sent anywhere. The app saves a few settings on your phone so it remembers them: your calibration, roof type and roofing material, slope units, code edition, stair and ramp type, the job and area you last worked in, and that you've seen the welcome screens. Deleting the app deletes them.

Jobs, photos and location (Pro)

If you use Jobs, Pitched saves each job on your phone: the name, client, address, map pin, property details you type in, your photos, notes and readings. Photos you take in Pitched are saved in the app, not in your photo library. Your company profile and logo, used on reports, are saved on your phone the same way. None of it is uploaded to us, and deleting a job or the app deletes it.

With your permission, Pitched uses your location only while the app is open, to pin the job and each entry on the map and fill in the address. It uses the camera only when you tap to take a photo.

To show maps and turn a location into an address, the app uses Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy. To find the county and city for the likely building department, the app sends only the job's map coordinates to the free U.S. Census Bureau geocoder (geocoding.geo.census.gov), under the Census Bureau's privacy policy. No name, address, photo or other job detail is sent with it.

Links that open in Safari

A job can open a web search for the county assessor's parcel search; the search holds only the county and state. Local roof advice can open the ASCE Hazard Tool. These open in Safari only when you tap them, and those sites' own privacy policies apply there.

Sharing

When you tap Share, or share a PDF report, iOS's share sheet sends it only to the app or person you choose. A report includes what you put in the job, such as the address, map, photos and your company details. Pitched doesn't see or keep a copy.

Purchases

Pitched Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether this Apple ID owns Pro.

Children

Pitched does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
